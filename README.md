# Google Cloud Professional Agentic Architect (PAA) — Part 1 Study Kit

Open companion resources for the Medium article *Preparing for the Google Cloud Professional Agentic Architect Exam: Study Process, Tips, and Traps to Avoid*.

All materials are unofficial study aids grounded exclusively in Google Cloud's publicly available [certification page](https://cloud.google.com/learn/certification/agentic-architect), [exam guide](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/professional_agentic_architect_exam_guide_english.pdf), [8-course Google Skills learning path](https://www.skills.google/paths/4525), public sample questions, and product documentation.
